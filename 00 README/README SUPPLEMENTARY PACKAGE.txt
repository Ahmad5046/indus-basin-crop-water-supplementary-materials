INDUS BASIN PAPER: SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIALS PACKAGE

Paper title
Metric-dependent crop water-management priorities in Pakistan's Indus Basin: Remote-sensing evidence for irrigation screening

Package date
2026-10-17

Contents
01_Supplementary_Document: Detailed supplementary methods, validation diagnostics, scenario tests, hotspot sensitivity, yield coverage, and numerical QC in Microsoft Word and PDF formats.
02_Supplementary_Tables: Tables S1-S10 plus the complete set of 20 machine-readable final analysis tables and the formatted Excel workbook.
03_Supplementary_Figures: Seventeen high-resolution PNG figures supporting district rankings, crop distributions, official-area comparison, provincial water use, temporal context, hotspot sensitivity, uncertainty, yield relationships, and crop contributions.
04_Analytical_Rasters: Forty GeoTIFF analytical outputs with the raster manifest and download-QC metadata.
05_Study_Area_Boundary: Study-area boundary in zipped ESRI Shapefile form, GeoJSON, and GeoPackage.
06_Reproducibility_Package: Modular GEE JavaScript, Python processing/audit code, machine-readable analysis data, execution metadata, and source-verification records.
07_QC_Provenance: Final project QC report, SHA-256 project inventory, final-table QC, and verified-reference workbook.

Interpretive boundaries
- The study area is the Pakistan portion of an Indus drainage-basin analysis boundary; it is not an official canal-command delineation.
- Crop areas are crop-season hectares and can exceed unique physical cultivated area where multiple cropping occurs.
- Green water is rainfall-attributed crop evapotranspiration. Blue water is residual evapotranspiration after effective rainfall and is not measured canal diversion, groundwater abstraction, or net depletion.
- Production-dependent VWF and water-productivity indicators are reported only where positive matched yield and mapped crop area are available. Missing denominators were not imputed.
- Hotspot and product-chain results are screening and structural-sensitivity diagnostics, not regulatory thresholds or confidence intervals.
