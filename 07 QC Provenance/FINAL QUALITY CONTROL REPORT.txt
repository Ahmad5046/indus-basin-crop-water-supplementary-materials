9. Equations and unit consistency: PASS
   - WaPOR dekadal AETI is multiplied by the catalog scale factor 0.1 and the
     actual number of days represented by each dekad.
   - Monthly effective rainfall uses the documented USDA-SCS relationship:
       Pe = P(125 - 0.2P) / 125, for P <= 250 mm
       Pe = 125 + 0.1P,       for P > 250 mm
   - Green water depth = min(actual ET, effective rainfall).
   - Blue water depth = max(actual ET - green water, 0).
   - Total crop water use = green water + blue water.
   - Volume (m3) = depth (mm) x mapped area (m2) / 1000.
   - Equivalently, 1 mm over 1 ha = 10 m3.
   - Mapped production (t) = mapped crop area (ha) x official yield (t/ha).
   - VWF (m3/t) = water volume (m3) / mapped production (t).
   - Water productivity (kg/m3) = mapped production (t) x 1000 / water (m3).

5. ASSUMPTIONS MADE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Study area is the Pakistan intersection of the selected Indus basin boundary.
- Karachi is represented as one complete city aggregate.
- Torghar and Tor Ghar are treated as the same district.
- Analysis year is 2023, with official crop statistics aligned to 2022-23.
- Crop-area totals are crop-season hectares and may exceed unique physical
  cropland where double cropping occurs.
- Mapped production equals mapped area multiplied by official district yield.
- Green water is the AETI portion met by USDA-SCS effective rainfall.
- Blue water is the residual AETI demand and is not a direct measurement of
  canal diversion or groundwater pumping.
- Dominant-crop VWF maps use the classified dominant crop and district yield.
- Hotspot and sustainability scores use basin-relative quartiles.
- The 10,000-draw uncertainty analysis is structural product sensitivity, not
  a statistical confidence interval.
- OSM 15 km canal-influence buffers provide context only.

  PageFit_Revision.docx. Keeping both files can cause submission ambiguity.
- The Word dataset table wraps long metadata into narrow columns. Text is not
  cropped, but the table is dense.
- The USDA-SCS effective-rainfall equation is empirical and is not locally
  calibrated for every district or soil/management condition.
- WaPOR, CHIRPS, MODIS, and ERA5-Land have product-specific uncertainty.
- Crop-class errors propagate into crop area, ET, production, VWF, and
  productivity.
- GMIA v5 is circa 2005 and is contextual rather than a current irrigation map.
- OSM infrastructure completeness varies spatially.
- Cartographic 500 m downloads are not substitutes for the authoritative 30 m
  crop maps or 250 m water-accounting assets.
- Legacy preliminary outputs and older helper scripts remain outside the final
  submission folder for provenance. They must not be mixed with final results.
