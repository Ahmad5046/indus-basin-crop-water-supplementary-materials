# Indus Basin crop-water supplementary materials

This repository contains the supplementary materials and reproducibility resources for the manuscript:

**Crop-water priorities depend on the accounting metric: Remote sensing evidence from Pakistan's Indus Basin**

## Repository contents

- `01 Supplementary Document`: detailed supplementary methods and quality-control information in DOCX and PDF formats.
- `02 Supplementary Tables`: Tables S1-S10 and the complete machine-readable analysis tables.
- `03 Supplementary Figures`: seventeen high-resolution supplementary figures and their captions.
- `04 Analytical Rasters`: forty GeoTIFF analytical outputs with raster metadata and QC records.
- `05 Study Area Boundary`: study-area boundary in ESRI Shapefile, GeoJSON, and GeoPackage formats.
- `06 Reproducibility Package`: Google Earth Engine JavaScript, Python scripts, data inputs, execution metadata, and audit records.
- `07 QC Provenance`: final quality-control reports, inventories, and verified-reference records.

## Interpretation

The study boundary represents the Pakistan portion of an Indus drainage-basin analysis boundary and is not an official canal-command delineation. Crop areas are crop-season hectares and may exceed unique physical cultivated area under multiple cropping. Green water denotes rainfall-attributed crop evapotranspiration. Blue water denotes residual evapotranspiration after effective rainfall and must not be interpreted as measured canal diversion, groundwater abstraction, or net depletion.

Production-dependent virtual-water-footprint and water-productivity indicators are reported only where positive matched yield and mapped crop area are available. Missing yield denominators were not imputed. Hotspot results are screening diagnostics rather than regulatory or sustainability thresholds.
